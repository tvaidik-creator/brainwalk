Subscriptions

BrainWalk starts with a 7-day free trial (up to 4 apps), then costs $5.99 per month or $59.99 per year with unlimited apps. Manage or cancel any time in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions on your iPhone. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Apps won't unlock?

  • Make sure BrainWalk has Health access: Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › BrainWalk.
  • Open BrainWalk once to refresh your step count.
  • Check that your trial or subscription is active in BrainWalk › Settings.

Stop using BrainWalk

Deleting the app removes all its restrictions. You can also turn off its Screen Time access in Settings › Screen Time.
