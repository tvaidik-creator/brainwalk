Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

BrainWalk ("we," "our," or "us") makes the BrainWalk iOS app (the "App"). This policy explains what information the App uses and how it is protected.

Information the App uses

Step data. With your permission, the App reads your step count from Apple Health to check progress toward your daily step goal. It only reads step data and never writes to Apple Health.

Screen Time selections. With your permission, the App uses Apple's Screen Time APIs (Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity) to lock and unlock the apps you choose. Apple gives the App opaque tokens for your selections, not the names of the apps you use or your browsing activity.

Subscription status. Purchases go through Apple's In-App Purchase system. We use RevenueCat to check whether you have an active subscription. We never see or store your payment details.

Your settings. Your step goal, unlock duration, walk history and similar preferences are stored on your device.

How we use it

  • To track your progress toward your daily step goal
  • To lock and unlock the apps you selected
  • To send the reminders and progress notifications you allow
  • To check whether you have an active trial or subscription

Storage and security

  • Health data is read on your device and is not sent to our servers.
  • App selections and settings are stored on your device in a shared app container.
  • Your Master Key PIN is hashed and stored in the iOS Keychain. We never store it in plain text and cannot recover it.
  • We do not run servers that store your personal data.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information, and we never use Health data for advertising. The only third-party service the App uses is RevenueCat, for subscription management. Its privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Your choices

  • Revoke Health access in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › BrainWalk.
  • Revoke Screen Time access in Settings › Screen Time.
  • Manage or cancel your subscription in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions.
  • Delete the App to remove all data it stored on your device.

Children

BrainWalk is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If we update this policy, we will post the new version here and change the date above.
